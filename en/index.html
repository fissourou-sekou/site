What makes the difference

A wall painted without preparation keeps its flaws under the paint. The steps of a careful repaint:

  1. Washing down dirty walls
  2. Filling holes and cracks
  3. Sanding and dusting
  4. Undercoat for an even colour
  5. Two coats of finish

Process

How a job works

Four steps, from the first visit to the final sweep.

  1. 1

    Visit

    Sékou comes to see the surfaces, takes measurements and advises you on colours and finishes.

  2. 2

    Written quote

    Areas in m², preparation, number of coats and products: every item is listed. You know what you are paying for.

  3. 3

    The work

    Floors and furniture protected, walls filled and sanded, undercoat, then two finishing coats.

  4. 4

    Handover

    You walk round the rooms together. Touch-ups are done on the spot and the site is left clean.

Advice

Matt, eggshell, satin or gloss?

The finish changes how a wall looks and how well it wears. Here is where each one works best.

  • Matt

    Sheen 1 of 4

    Absorbs light and hides small flaws in the wall.

    Ceilings, living rooms, bedrooms

  • Eggshell

    Sheen 2 of 4

    A soft sheen, easier to clean than matt.

    Bedrooms, hallways, living areas

  • Satin

    Sheen 3 of 4

    Stands up to moisture and repeated washing.

    Kitchens, bathrooms, woodwork

  • Gloss

    Sheen 4 of 4

    Very hard-wearing and bright, but it shows the slightest flaw in the surface.

    Doors, skirting boards, metalwork

Questions

What clients often ask

Another question? Ask Fissourou Peinture directly.

Is the quote free?

Yes. Sékou comes to see the surfaces, then gives you a detailed written quote, with no obligation.

How is the price worked out?

It depends on the area to paint, the condition of the walls and the finish you choose. A cracked wall needs more preparation than a sound one. Every item is on the quote: preparation, number of coats, products.

Who supplies the paint?

Sékou can supply professional paint suited to each room and each surface. If you have already bought your own, just mention it when you ask for the quote.

Do the rooms need to be emptied?

Remove fragile items and small furniture. Everything else is grouped in the middle of the room and covered with dust sheets, like the floors.

Can you stay at home during the work?

Yes. Rooms are done one after another. Water-based paints, the most common indoors, have little smell and dry quickly.

How long does a job take?

Mostly it depends on the preparation and the drying time between coats. The expected duration is on the quote, along with the start date.

Does he hang wallpaper and murals?

Yes: standard or non-woven wallpaper, panoramic murals and illustrated murals. You choose the design; Sékou prepares the wall, then hangs it strip by strip, taking care over the pattern matching.

Does he work for managing agents, landlords and estate agents?

Yes: communal areas in buildings, refurbishment between tenants, and refreshing a home before it is sold or let.
